Which AI agents are hitting your site - and what are they reading?

Upload the server logs you already have. Signal Traffic reads them in your browser and shows you every AI crawler and agent that visited: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot and 30+ more - with the exact pages each one read.

What it does: parses Apache, nginx and most JSON log formats line by line, matches each request against a database of known AI and search crawlers, and builds a report: hits per agent, pages read, status codes, first and last visit.

What it does not do: upload your logs anywhere, connect to your server, or guess. It reads only the log lines you give it, in this tab. If a visitor is not in the log, it is not in the report. Close the tab and nothing is kept.
